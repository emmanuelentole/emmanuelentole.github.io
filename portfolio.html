CLINICAL & ACADEMIC DEVELOPMENT

Dental
Portfolio.

Developing clinical knowledge, skills and professional judgement.

This portfolio presents selected areas of my undergraduate dental education at the University of Pretoria. It reflects my learning, clinical exposure and developing approach to patient-centred care.

← Back to homepage

01 — PAEDIATRIC DENTISTRY

Building healthy smiles early.

My paediatric dentistry training has involved assessing the oral health needs of children, identifying dental caries and understanding appropriate treatment options for primary teeth.

Areas of learning include behaviour management, pulp therapy, restorative treatment and preventive oral healthcare.

Key focus: Prevention, child-friendly communication and evidence-based treatment planning.

02 — ORTHODONTICS

Understanding occlusion and growth.

My orthodontic experience has introduced me to the assessment of malocclusions, dental development and the relationship between oral habits and occlusal problems.

Areas of learning include anterior open bite, posterior crossbite, digit-sucking habits, interceptive orthodontics and removable appliance principles.

Key focus: Diagnosis, growth and development, and appropriate orthodontic intervention.

03 — PROSTHODONTICS

Restoring function and aesthetics.

My prosthodontic training has developed my understanding of treatment planning for fixed and removable prostheses, material selection and the importance of occlusion.

Areas of learning include fixed dental prostheses, preparation principles, ceramic materials, prosthetic design and occlusal assessment.

Key focus: Function, aesthetics, material selection and treatment planning.

04 — ENDODONTICS

Preserving natural teeth.

My endodontic training has focused on understanding pulpal and periapical disease, diagnosis and the principles of root canal treatment.

Areas of learning include endodontic diagnosis, access cavity principles, canal preparation, irrigation, obturation and post-treatment restoration.

Key focus: Diagnosis, infection control and preservation of natural dentition.

05 — PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT

Always learning.

I am committed to continuous learning, improving my clinical skills and developing as a dental professional.
